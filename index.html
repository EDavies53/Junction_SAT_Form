The commissioning of traffic signals can be divided into three parts.
1.The EPROM test of the controller
2.The checks that can be carried out before the controller is switched on
3.The checks that can be carried out after the controller is switched on
Note:- UTC Site - Remember to print off the relevant OTU Details / Commissioning Sheet and Control Reply Sheet
Part 1 should be carried out in accordance with TR2500A data sheets and is not covered in this document
Part 2 can be carried out in the absence of the signal company Engineer and it is suggested that this is carried out before they arrive on site. One hour upwards should be allowed for this.
Part 3 should be carried out in the presence of a signal company Engineer
